## 9. What this means for the modelling pipeline

1. **Split on `lesion_id`, never on image rows.** Every lesion has exactly two images of the same skin. A random row split leaks 5,240 lesions across train and test and inflates every score.
2. **Do not report plain accuracy.** With 69.4% of lesions in one class, a constant prediction beats most honest models on accuracy. Use balanced accuracy, macro-F1, per-class recall and, above all, **sensitivity on malignant lesions**.
3. **The 11-class target is severely imbalanced (280:1).** Classes such as VASC, BEN_OTH, MAL_OTH have only 47, 44, 9 lesions — expect to need class weighting, oversampling or merged categories.
4. **Two modalities, two pixel distributions.** Dermoscopic and clinical images differ measurably in colour and contrast. Treat `image_type` as a first-class feature: train separate heads, condition on it, or at minimum stratify by it.
5. **Metadata is not free.** `anatom_site_general` is missing for 37% of images and `melanocytic` for 77%. Any metadata feature needs an explicit missing-value strategy.
6. **Report fairness by skin tone.** Skin tone class III is 61% of the data; the extremes are almost absent. Aggregate metrics will hide failures on under-sampled groups.
